# Record of processing activities (Art. 30)

Controller: the operator of the service (for the hosted service, Omni Edge SRL), contact in the privacy notice. Data protection officer: none appointed; reassess when processing is at large scale (Art. 37).

| Activity | Data subjects | Data | Purpose and basis | Recipients | Transfers | Retention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accounts | Users | Email, password hash, consent records, sign-in records | Make and secure the account. Contract; legitimate interest | Hosting; email provider (messages only) | EU | Until the account is deleted; sign-in records (browser and address) at most 14 days, deleted hourly; a hash of each browser's cookie and the date it was first seen, at most a year (the cookie's life), deleted hourly |
| Records | Users, and the people the records are about (the user, or a child or relative managed by them) | Uploaded documents, text read from them, extracted details, notes, tags, doctors named, conversations with the assistant. Read and kept on the user's device; the server holds them only as sealed objects it cannot open | Store, organise, search, translate and share the user's records. Explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)) | Hosting (sealed objects only); the relay, which sees the plaintext of a model call while it runs it and stores and logs none; the model provider for the AI features the instance has switched on (the text or page of a request only); people the user shares with | EU endpoints; none outside the EU unless the operator configures one | Until deleted by the user; an object the vault removed stays a week in the backup, the daily snapshots are kept seven by default, and an account's own copy in the backup goes at once when it is deleted (for a bucket, its own versioning, with a lifecycle rule that expires old versions) |
| Sharing | Users; recipients | Sealed share copies (the key is in the link's address and never reaches the server), their end time, and a record of each open: time, country and the document opened (for a link that needs approval also the visitor's note and public key, until the owner's device collects them) | Let the user share and see who read it. Contract; legitimate interest | Hosting | EU | Until the link or account is deleted |
| Plans and payment (only with `SPINE_BILLING=on`) | Users who buy a plan | Which plan, the payment provider's customer and subscription identifiers, when it renews or ends, how much AI processing was used in the period, for reading, chat and translation, an estimate of what it cost (numbers), the totals of pages read, questions answered and pages translated since the account began (three numbers), how many times the share links were opened and how many bytes they sent (numbers), and for two days a random id of each chat question and each translation. No card, name or billing address: the provider keeps those | Sell and give the plan chosen. Contract | The payment provider (Paddle, seller of record: it receives what the buyer types on its page) | Per the provider's own terms; to be confirmed | While the account exists; the provider's own records as it states |
| Shared plan (seats; only with `SPINE_BILLING=on`) | Users who pay for a plan with seats; users who hold a seat in it | Which accounts share a plan (by account), when each joined, waiting invitations (an end date and a hash of the link's secret), when an account last gave up a seat and in whose plan. The account that pays learns who holds a seat and how many people the plan holds, nothing of what a member keeps | Give the members the plan, let either side end a seat, keep seats from being passed from plan to plan (a wait of 30 days). Contract | Hosting; the mail provider (a notice to the account that pays and to the member when a seat is taken or ended) | EU | Until the seat ends or either account is deleted; the note of when an account gave a seat up is kept until it takes a seat again or is deleted |
| Follow-up reminders | Users; guardians and the people themselves let in to a profile | For each reminder the owner confirmed: an id, the due date, the profile it is of, whether its mail was sent. The words stay in the sealed records | Mail a neutral line on the day to the account and to those looking after the profile. Contract; consent when a reminder is confirmed | Hosting; the mail provider (an address and a line that says a reminder is due) | EU | Until the reminder is changed, done or deleted, or the account is deleted |
| Emergency card | Users; people who open a card | The card the user wrote (sealed on their device, bundle kept at the server, which cannot read it), optionally with official numbers the user chose to put on it; the time and country of each opening (never the address) until the owner's device collects them; the date it was last sent or checked, for a yearly reminder email | Let someone helping the user read what the user chose to show. Explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)); the user turns it on after being told that anyone with the code reads it | Hosting (sealed bytes); the mail provider (the reminder and the notice of an opening, neither naming anything on the card) | EU | Until the card is turned off or the account deleted; the opening record until the owner's device collects it |
| People and access for others | Users; the people whose records they keep (children, relatives); people let in | Which accounts may open which person's records, their role, when; a child's birth date, sealed with the records and unreadable to the server; sealed keys. Invitations (a hash, not the code) for a week | Let a parent, a guardian or a person themselves open records. Contract; the guardian's invitation is the consent for a young person from sixteen (CR-FAM-01) | Hosting; the mail provider (a notice to the owner when an invitation is accepted or a profile is taken over) | EU | Until access is ended or the account deleted |
| Waiting list and counts | People who ask to be told when Spine opens; visitors | An address and the day, and where the link said they came from; counts a day of sign-ups (and the loop they came from), shared pages and cards opened, and checkouts started, joined to no one | Tell people who asked once, when it opens (consent, Art. 6(1)(a)); see whether the service works (legitimate interest) | Hosting | EU | An address until it is removed on request; counts without limit, since they identify no one |
| Demo uploads (only with `SPINE_DEMO_UPLOADS=on`) | Visitors with no account, and the people their files are about | The text of up to a few files' first pages, and images of pages with no text where image reading is allowed, sent from the visitor's browser through the relay to the model provider; the relay's day count of calls for a keyed hash of the visitor's address. Nothing is kept by the service | Let someone try Spine on their own documents. Explicit consent, asked before the first file (Art. 9(2)(a)) | The relay; the model provider | As for Records (the provider's EU endpoint) | Not kept; the files live in the visitor's page, and if they choose to keep them, in their browser for a day at most; the count is kept for the day |
| Security | Users; visitors | Rate-limit counters, application log lines without content; the relay's count of model calls per account and per share | Protect the service. Legitimate interest | Hosting; the relay | EU | Up to 12 months |

Technical and organisational measures: SECURITY.md (records encrypted on the device with keys the server never has; the server derives nothing from the password; sign-in on a separately derived value hashed with argon2id; optional second factor and passkeys; TLS; ciphertext-only backups; dependency and secret scanning in CI). Nothing here has been audited.
