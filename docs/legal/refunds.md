# Refund policy

This page says how refunds work for the paid plans and the page top-up of this Spine service, which is run by {{operator}} ("we"). Questions about a purchase: {{contact}}. It goes with the [terms of use](/terms), whose section on paid plans it explains; where the two differ, the terms apply.

## Who sells, and who pays you back

Paddle sells the plans and the top-up to you as the seller of record: it takes the payment, issues the invoice and accounts for tax, and its own terms apply to the purchase. A refund is paid back by Paddle to the way you paid. Where {{operator}} sells them instead, through Stripe, we pay the refund back to the way you paid, and the invoice is reversed with a credit invoice (a storno), which is sent to you in the same way.

## The fourteen days

Where we sell, you can withdraw from your account page: within the fourteen days press "Withdraw from this purchase". We confirm by email at once, and answer within fourteen days. If we accept, we end your plan and pay back to the way you paid; for a plan you have already used we pay back less the part of the service already given, as the law allows. Where Paddle sells, you ask Paddle or us.

For a digital service you have fourteen days to withdraw from a purchase, counted from the day you buy. You do not have to give a reason. If you ask for the service to start earlier, you lose that right as the law allows, as the order page says.

For a yearly plan, if you withdraw within those fourteen days we refund what you paid, less what the law allows us to keep for the part of the service already given.

## After the fourteen days

You can cancel at any time from your account, on the payment provider's page. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep the plan until the end of what you paid for. What you have already paid for a period that has begun is not paid back, unless the law gives you a right to it or we charged you by mistake.

## A charge that is wrong

If you were charged twice, or after you cancelled, write to us and we will correct it. Say the address of your account, the date of the charge and the number on the invoice.

## What we need, and what we do not

Do not send us any document, record or medical detail. Your records are encrypted so that we cannot read them, and a refund never needs them.

## Your records

When a subscription ends, or is refunded, nothing is deleted: your records stay, you can export them, and the free plan's limits apply to what you add and to the features you use.

## Your rights

Nothing on this page limits the rights you have as a consumer, including under the law of the country where you live.
