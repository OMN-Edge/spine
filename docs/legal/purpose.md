# What Spine is for

Spine is a place to keep your own health documents, organise them, search them, have them translated and show them to a doctor you choose.

It does four things with a document: it **stores** it, it **organises** it (it reads out the dates, names, values and headings the document itself contains, and groups documents that belong together), it **translates** what the document says, and it **shares** what you choose with someone you choose, for a time you choose.

It does not do anything else. In particular Spine does not diagnose, does not monitor your health, does not predict or prevent anything, does not suggest or change a treatment, does not tell you whether a result is good or bad, and does not decide who should be seen or when. Where it restates a document (a summary, a translation, an answer to a question about your records) it only restates what the document says, and says so; the document itself is the record and is always one tap away.

Spine is therefore not a medical device and is not meant for emergencies or for deciding on care. Ask your doctor about what your records mean.
