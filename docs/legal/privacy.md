# Privacy notice

## What we process, and why

| What | Why | On what basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your email address and a hash of your password | To make and secure your account and to send you account messages | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) |
| The documents you upload, what is read from them (dates, providers, names, values, summaries), your notes and tags, and the conversations you have with the assistant | To store, organise, search, translate and share your records, as you ask | Your explicit consent to the use of your health data (Art. 9(2)(a)); you give it separately from accepting the terms and can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account |

| Sign-in records (time, browser, address) | To protect your account and show you where it is signed in | Our legitimate interest in security |

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or use it to train models. We make no decision about you by automated means.

## Who else sees it

- **Hosting.** Your records are kept on infrastructure we run or rent in the European Union.
- **AI models.** When a feature uses a model (reading a scan that has no text, extracting details, summarising, translating, answering your questions), the text, or for a scan the page image, that the feature needs is sent to the model provider this service is set up with, for that request only, at an endpoint in the European Union, under terms that do not allow the provider to keep it or train on it. You can see which features do this on your account page; some are switched off by default.
- **Email.** Account messages (verifying an address, a reset link, a notice that a share was requested) go through an email provider. They never contain a document or a result.
- **People you share with.** A share link shows what you chose to whoever opens it.
- Authorities, only where the law requires it.

## How long, and how safe

Records stay until you delete them or your account. Deleting the account removes the records and the files; backups made before that are overwritten within the retention period (a few weeks). Sign-in and access records are kept as long as needed for security and no longer than twelve months.

Passwords are stored as slow hashes; the connection is encrypted; sign-ins can be protected with a second factor; every query is limited to the account's own records; share links are random, expire and can be revoked.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to limit or object to its processing, and to receive it in a machine-readable form. The account page lets you **export everything** and **delete your account** yourself, immediately. You can withdraw your consent at any time (withdrawing ends the use of your health data, so it means deleting the account); this does not affect what was done before. You can complain to the supervisory authority: in Romania the ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro), or the one where you live.

## Children

An account for a child is managed by a parent or guardian. A young person under 16 needs their guardian's consent.

## Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, you are told and asked again.
